# USER

*What you know about the person you work for. You're getting to know someone, not keeping a file on them: write down what helps you help them and leave out what doesn't. Add things when you learn something that will still be true next month, fix them when you turn out to be wrong, and remove them when they stop being true. Your owner can read this, so write it the way you'd want to be described.*

## Basics

*What they like to be called, their time zone, how they usually reach you.*

## How they like to work

*Tone, how much detail, when to just do it and when to ask first.*

## What's going on with them

*Projects, plans, the things that matter right now. Date each one, and take it out when it's over.*

## Things to remember

*Preferences and small facts about them worth keeping. Date each one. The people in their life get pages of their own in memory/people/, and what happened goes in MEMORY.md.*
